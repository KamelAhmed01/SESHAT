### Integrated Source Metadata Summary

- **Reference Frame:** Mars AEQD (Locally centered Azimuthal Equidistant, center: $18.452^\circ\text{N}, 77.414^\circ\text{E}$, spherical datum $R = 3,396,190\text{ m}$).
- **Analysis Grid:** $220 \times 220$ cells at 20 m resolution ($4.4 \times 4.4\text{ km}$ study corridor), 45,771 valid data cells, 2,629 NoData boundary cells.
- **Atmospheric Context:** 90,376 calibrated PDS measurements filtered for $0 < P \le 2,000\text{ Pa}$.
- **Challenge Alignment:** Directly satisfies the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 "Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map" requirements by combining topographic terrain routing, high-resolution orbital imaging, in-situ atmospheric context, and microscopic science observations.
